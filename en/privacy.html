Privacy Policy

For the Lieslo app and this website · Last updated: 25 November 2026 (app version 1.7)

In short: Lieslo processes your notifications only on your phone. The app has no internet permission, sends no data to us or to third parties, and contains no ads and no tracking.

1. The Lieslo app

1.1 Notifications

To read messages aloud and filter them, you grant Lieslo notification access. Lieslo reads the content of incoming notifications (e.g. app, sender, text) and uses your rules to decide whether to read it aloud, show it or hide it. This processing happens only on your device. Lieslo has no internet permission and technically cannot send this data anywhere.

1.2 Data stored on your device

  • Rules and settings you create (e.g. names of people or groups in a rule).
  • Your pronunciation list (“word → say it like this”), if you add entries under “Adjust pronunciation”. Lieslo only uses it when reading aloud.
  • Your profiles, time windows and quiet time (see 1.6).
  • Your reply texts and a record of who was answered automatically and when (see 1.5). This record is deleted after 24 hours, and immediately when you turn off automatic replies.
  • A history of notifications so you can see what you missed – together with replies sent automatically or by dictation. Entries are deleted automatically after the time you set (1, 12 or 24 hours, 24 hours by default; at most 5,000 entries). You can turn the history off in the settings; this deletes the existing history immediately.
  • A technical error log without message contents, names or senders (deleted after 7 days, at most 100 entries).

When is data deleted? Lieslo checks these periods in the background every hour and whenever a new notification arrives – even if you don’t open the app for days. Expired data is therefore usually deleted within an hour after the period ends. If you revoke Lieslo’s notification access, Lieslo no longer runs in the background; no new data is added then, and expired data is deleted the next time you open the app. Dictated replies are stored only as part of the history; the voice recording itself is never stored.

This data is not backed up to any cloud and not transferred to a new device. Uninstalling Lieslo or clearing its app data removes it completely.

1.3 Reading aloud

To read aloud, Lieslo uses your phone’s text-to-speech engine (e.g. from Google or Samsung) or another text-to-speech app you choose. The text is passed to that app on your device. How that app processes data is governed by its provider’s terms.

1.4 Microphone (dictate a reply)

If you turn on “Dictate a reply”, Lieslo asks for permission to use the microphone. The microphone is used only while you dictate a reply, and only while the dictation window is open. Your speech is turned into text by your phone’s speech recognition, directly on the device. Lieslo does not store or transmit any audio recording. The recognised text appears only in the history on your phone (24 hours at most). You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Android settings; everything else keeps working without it.

1.5 Automatic and dictated replies

Replies are not sent by Lieslo but by your messenger app: Lieslo fills in the “Reply” button that the messenger offers in its notification. The messenger’s own privacy policy applies to sending. A dictated reply is sent only after you confirm it. Automatic replies are off until you turn them on; you choose the text and the people yourself. Your reply texts and the record of who was answered and when stay on your phone only.

1.6 Profiles, time windows and quiet time

Profiles, time windows and quiet time are stored on your phone only. To switch profiles automatically, Lieslo checks on the phone what time it is, whether headphones are connected and the name of the connected audio device. None of this leaves the phone.

1.7 Bug reports (optional)

With “Share error report” you can send us a technical report (device model, Android and app version, settings as numbers, recent error messages). It contains no messages, names or senders. You see it before sending and send it yourself, e.g. by email. We use it only to fix bugs and delete it when it is no longer needed for that. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working app) or Art. 6(1)(b) if you ask us for help.

1.8 Buying Lieslo Pro and ratings

Purchases of Lieslo Pro are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive payment details. For purchases, Google only provides us with the information needed for accounting (e.g. order number, product, country, price). Lieslo checks the purchase status with the Google Play app on your device. If Lieslo asks you for a rating, this uses Google Play’s rating feature; we only see your rating as it appears publicly in the store. Processing by Google is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

1.9 Permissions

  • Notification access: to read aloud and filter (see 1.1).
  • Post notifications: so Lieslo can show filtered messages, the active profile and the button for dictating a reply.
  • Microphone (optional): only to dictate a reply (see 1.4).
  • Google Play billing: to buy Lieslo Pro.
  • Folder for voice messages (optional): Using the Android folder picker, you can give Lieslo access to a folder, e.g. “WhatsApp Voice Notes”. Lieslo only reads voice messages there to play them after the announcement. The files stay on your device. You can revoke access at any time.
  • List of apps: Lieslo can see which apps with a launcher icon and which text-to-speech apps are installed, so you can choose them in Lieslo. This list stays on your device.

1.10 Children

Lieslo is not specifically directed at children.

1.11 Recommending Lieslo

With “Recommend Lieslo”, Lieslo opens Android’s share sheet with a short text and the link to Lieslo on Google Play. You choose which app to use and who to send it to; that app sends it. Lieslo does not learn whether or with whom you shared it.

2. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, to deliver the page and keep it secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads no third-party content (even the font is hosted on this website).

3. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed for that. This also applies to “Report a translation error” in the app’s settings: Lieslo opens your email app with our address, a subject containing the app version and your language (e.g. “pt-BR”) and a short template. You see everything before sending and send the email yourself.

4. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Contact us at lieslo.adress@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the country or state where you live.

Because the app does not transfer your data to us, we have no access to your rules, history or messages. You can delete them yourself at any time in the app or by uninstalling it.

5. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
