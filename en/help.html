Help

Answers to common questions about Lieslo.

Lieslo doesn’t read anything – what can I do?
  1. In Lieslo, open Settings → Is everything working?. It shows whether a permission is missing.
  2. Check that notification access is allowed for Lieslo.
  3. Turn up the media volume. If it is all the way down, you won’t hear anything.
  4. Check that an enabled rule matches the message.
  5. Allow Lieslo to run in the background (see battery settings below).
Battery settings by manufacturer

Some manufacturers stop apps in the background to save battery. Lieslo may then miss messages. Menu names can differ slightly depending on your Android version.

  • Samsung: Settings → Apps → Lieslo → Battery → Unrestricted.
  • Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO: Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Lieslo → Battery saver → No restrictions; also turn on Autostart.
  • Huawei, Honor: Settings → Battery → App launch → Lieslo → Manage manually and turn all switches on.
  • OnePlus, OPPO, realme: Settings → Battery → Lieslo → Allow background activity or Don’t optimize.
  • Others: Settings → Apps → Lieslo → Battery → Don’t optimize or Unrestricted.

Lieslo also shows you this help in the app when you first start it.

How do I get more natural voices?

Lieslo uses your phone’s text-to-speech. To make it sound more natural:

  1. Install Speech Services by Google from the Play Store and download the voices for your languages there.
  2. Or use a free text-to-speech app such as SherpaTTS (available via F-Droid).
  3. Then choose the app in Lieslo under Settings → Text-to-speech app.

If a language has no voice, download it in your text-to-speech app.

Why aren’t one-time codes (e.g. SMS codes) read aloud?

Starting with Android 15, Android no longer passes such codes to apps like Lieslo for security reasons. This is intentional and protects you.

How do I read aloud only at certain times? (Time windows and quiet time)

These features are part of Lieslo Pro.

  • Time window per rule: Open the rule and add a time window under Only at certain times. Choose weekdays and a time from–to, e.g. Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00. Windows across midnight work too (e.g. 22:00–07:00). A rule can have several time windows.
  • Quiet time: Settings → Read aloud → Quiet time. Choose when Lieslo reads nothing aloud, for example at night. Under Exceptions you pick rules that still read aloud (e.g. “Mom”). You can also do this in the rule with Also read aloud during quiet time.

The filter keeps showing matching notifications outside these times. The history shows why a message was not read aloud.

What are profiles?

With profiles (Lieslo Pro) you decide which rules apply right now, e.g. different ones in the car than at work. Without profiles everything stays as it is (profile “Default”).

  • Create: Settings → Read aloud → Profiles. Name, icon, which rules are on, plus settings such as “headphones only”, quiet time and speed.
  • Switch: on the home screen, with the Quick Settings tile Lieslo profile (pull the notification bar all the way down → Edit → add the tile) or with Lieslo’s “Profile: …” notification.
  • Automatically: in the profile under Switch on automatically: when a certain device is connected (e.g. the car radio), at certain times or when headphones are connected. Lieslo shows which profile applies right now and why.
How do automatic replies work?

Lieslo can answer messages with a fixed text, e.g. “I’m driving, I’ll get back to you later.” (Lieslo Pro). The reply is sent by your messenger app through the “Reply” button in its notification. Lieslo itself has no internet access.

  1. Settings → Read aloud → Automatic replies: turn on the main switch and choose how often the same person can get a reply at most (default: 1 hour).
  2. In a rule (or a profile), turn on Reply automatically and choose the text.

Protection: at most one reply per person in the chosen period, never in groups (unless you choose Also reply in groups), never to your own messages, calls, system notifications or one-time codes. The history marks every automatic reply. The main switch or “Switch off” on the home screen stops everything immediately.

Not every app offers a “Reply” button. In the app list, suitable apps show Automatic replies possible.

How do I dictate a reply?

This feature is part of Lieslo Pro and needs Android 12 or newer.

  1. Turn on Settings → Read aloud → Dictate a reply and allow the microphone.
  2. After reading a chat message aloud, Lieslo shows a quiet notification with a Reply button.
  3. Tap it and speak. Lieslo reads your reply back (“Reply to …: … Send?”).
  4. Only when you tap Send does the messenger send the reply. Optional: Confirm with “Yes”.

Microphone: Lieslo only needs it for dictating, and only while the dictation window is open. Speech is recognised on the phone only; nothing is stored or sent. You can withdraw the permission at any time: phone settings → Apps → Lieslo → Permissions → Microphone.

Feature locked? Then the language pack for on-device speech recognition is missing. Tap Download language pack in Lieslo. Which languages are available depends on your phone.

I bought Pro. Is it gone after reinstalling?

No. Google Play remembers the purchase in your Google account. Lieslo restores it automatically when you open the app. If not: Settings → Lieslo Pro → Restore purchase. Use the same Google account you bought it with.

How do I report a bug?

In Lieslo, go to Settings → Share error report. The report only contains technical details, no messages and no names, and you can see it before sending. Or email us at lieslo.adress@gmail.com.
