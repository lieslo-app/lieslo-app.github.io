Help

Answers to common questions about Lieslo.

Lieslo doesn’t read anything – what can I do?
  1. In Lieslo, open Settings → Is everything working?. It shows whether a permission is missing.
  2. Check that notification access is allowed for Lieslo.
  3. Turn up the media volume. If it is all the way down, you won’t hear anything.
  4. Check that an enabled rule matches the message.
  5. Allow Lieslo to run in the background (see battery settings below).
Battery settings by manufacturer

Some manufacturers stop apps in the background to save battery. Lieslo may then miss messages. Menu names can differ slightly depending on your Android version.

  • Samsung: Settings → Apps → Lieslo → Battery → Unrestricted.
  • Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO: Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Lieslo → Battery saver → No restrictions; also turn on Autostart.
  • Huawei, Honor: Settings → Battery → App launch → Lieslo → Manage manually and turn all switches on.
  • OnePlus, OPPO, realme: Settings → Battery → Lieslo → Allow background activity or Don’t optimize.
  • Others: Settings → Apps → Lieslo → Battery → Don’t optimize or Unrestricted.

Lieslo also shows you this help in the app when you first start it.

How do I get more natural voices?

Lieslo uses your phone’s text-to-speech. To make it sound more natural:

  1. Install Speech Services by Google from the Play Store and download the voices for your languages there.
  2. Or use a free text-to-speech app such as SherpaTTS (available via F-Droid).
  3. Then choose the app in Lieslo under Settings → Text-to-speech app.

If a language has no voice, download it in your text-to-speech app.

Why aren’t one-time codes (e.g. SMS codes) read aloud?

Starting with Android 15, Android no longer passes such codes to apps like Lieslo for security reasons. This is intentional and protects you.

I bought Pro. Is it gone after reinstalling?

No. Google Play remembers the purchase in your Google account. Lieslo restores it automatically when you open the app. If not: Settings → Lieslo Pro → Restore purchase. Use the same Google account you bought it with.

How do I report a bug?

In Lieslo, go to Settings → Share error report. The report only contains technical details, no messages and no names, and you can see it before sending. Or email us at lieslo.adress@gmail.com.
