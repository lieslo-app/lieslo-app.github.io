Everything you need. Nothing that annoys.

Lieslo is deliberately simple, so it works without a long learning curve.

Only what matters to you

Simple rules decide which apps, people or groups get read aloud. Everything else stays quiet.

Filter out the noise

Hide unimportant notifications of an app. The important ones stay visible.

The right voice

Lieslo detects the language of a message and picks a matching voice. Each rule can have its own voice if you like.

Works with almost any app

WhatsApp, Snapchat, Instagram, SMS, email and many more: Lieslo works with apps that show notifications.

Never miss a thing

The history shows your notifications from the last 24 hours, stored only on your phone.

Ready in a minute

Templates like “Read aloud certain people” make getting started easy, no tech skills needed.

Your messages stay with you.

Lieslo doesn’t even have permission to access the internet. What Lieslo reads never leaves your phone.

  • No internet access
  • No ads, no tracking
  • No account, no sign-up
  • History is deleted after 24 hours
